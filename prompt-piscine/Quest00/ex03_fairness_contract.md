# The Fairness Contract
## I will use AI when:

I have already made a genuine attempt to solve the problem myself.

I want a deeper explanation of why something works the way it does.

I am looking to compare different methods or explore alternative solutions.

## I will NOT use AI when:

I have not first struggled with the problem on my own.

I am taking an exam or completing a formal assessment.

I am still building foundational knowledge that requires personal practice.

## I know I am using AI fairly when:

I can explain my code or reasoning without relying on AI.

I am capable of solving a similar task independently.

My confidence in my understanding has genuinely increased.
