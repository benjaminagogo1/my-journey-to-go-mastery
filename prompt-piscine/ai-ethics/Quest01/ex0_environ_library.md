# I will use AI to:

Deepen my understanding, not just collect answers.

Challenge my assumptions and force me to think more critically.

Explore complex issues — especially historical and ethical ones — beyond my current level of knowledge.

Work more efficiently only after I have built a solid foundation myself.

# I will NOT use AI to:

Escape the productive struggle that builds real competence.

Present answers as mine when I have not fully understood or worked through them.

Skip foundational skills that require discipline and repetition.

Outsource my thinking, judgment, or responsibility.
